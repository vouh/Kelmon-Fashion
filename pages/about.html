Who We Are

EzyBite is a student-powered snack delivery service dedicated to bringing hot, freshly made samosas and snacks straight to your door — fast and affordable. We started with a simple idea: no one should have to leave their room or walk across campus just to get a delicious bite.

Whether you're buried in assignments, between lectures, or just craving something crispy — we've got you.

1. 0–2s — Logo reveal: Your glam era starts here.

4. 8–11s — Man applies perfume: Smell unforgettable.

## CapCut assembly

1. Create a new **1080 × 1920, 30 fps** project.
2. Import the six PNG files from `video-frames` in filename order.
3. Set durations to **2s, 3s, 3s, 3s, 2s, 2s**.
4. Add a short **Dissolve** transition (about 0.15–0.25 seconds) between scenes.
5. Apply a very gentle zoom-in to every scene; keep faces, the bag and perfume bottle inside the safe area.
6. Add an upbeat licensed Afro-pop or stylish instrumental track and align each cut to a beat.
7. Export as **MP4, H.264, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps**.
8. Use the same export for Instagram Reels, TikTok and WhatsApp Status.
